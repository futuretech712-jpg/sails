Seamanship

You cannot sail straight into the wind. Watch the pennant and the teal mark on the compass, and zigzag about 45° either side to go upwind. Red marks on the compass are enemy ships. Skeletons guard buried treasure. Sharks hunt swimmers in deep water. Restock planks and cannonballs at the crates on the Gullhaven pier.
